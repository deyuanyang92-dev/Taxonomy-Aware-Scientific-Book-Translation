- `чифт` / `цифт` (pl. `чифта` / `цифта`) = **pair**. "два чифта тентакуларни цируси" = "two PAIRS of tentacular cirri"
  (four cirri). `цифт` is the same word (variant spelling in this text). NEVER remove "pair(s)" from the translation
  for these words. (A cheap model once deleted "pairs" in 12 places, e.g. "Four pairs of tentacular cirri" -> "Four
  tentacular cirri"; all 12 were wrong.)
- `нечифтен` / `нечифтна` = **unpaired** (median), e.g. нечифтна антена = median (unpaired) antenna.
- `два пъти по-къси` = "half as long" (literally "twice shorter"); "half as long" is the correct English, not an error.
- `ротатории` = rotifers (Rotifera); "rotifers" is correct English, do not change it to "rotatorians".
